Why I bothered
with the small stuff.

Getting the lights right is the useful part. I also want to enjoy the small adjustments along the way. Letting go of a point should feel finished; changing a shape should be easy to follow.

That is what I’m after with these “joy animations” in Lightener Studio. A point settling or a line lighting up makes the edit feel more responsive to my hand. As soon as I grab the next point, I want to keep editing.

About these motion studies

The four studies show each effect at a larger scale. The card above is the actual Lightener Studio editor with demo lights. New demo lights use prepared curves to make their entrances easier to see. Motion options are off by default in the product. The “Motion off for this page” controls also turn them off in the card; Pause autoplay only stops the automatic previews.

Production timing: settle 70 + 180 ms; line feedback 400 ms; ripple at most 440 ms; draw-in 500 ms, followed by staggered point pops. The studies use simplified geometry.
